the One App

One app to rule them all, one app to find them,
one app to bring them all, and in the network bind them.

The vision for this project is simple: make using AI to its full potential easy.

I want

  • Any AI assistant of mine, from anywhere, to act as me across all my devices and accounts, with nothing walled off.
  • It to be fast.
  • Nothing to belong to one AI provider. If I switch, or use several at once, nothing is lost.
  • Whatever software we need, by asking for it. My AI makes it, and any of us can have it changed when it doesn't fit.

Early days. This changes every day and things break. I wouldn't use it or rely on it yet; wait until it settles.
