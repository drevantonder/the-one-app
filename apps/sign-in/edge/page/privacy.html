Privacy

This network is a private network that belongs to its owner. Only they and the people they invite use it. It isn't a public service.

Who's responsible

This network is run by its owner, who is responsible for the data kept in it.

What's kept, and where

What people keep here, and what the network's apps keep for them, is stored in the network's own storage bucket and on its servers, which its owner and its people run. Accounts and passwords someone adds are kept in their Vault.

Google accounts

If you sign a Google account in to this network's Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs or Google Sheets:

  • Your Google data is used only by those apps, to do what you, or an AI assistant you let act as you, ask of them, for you.
  • It isn't sold, shared with anyone else or used for advertising.
  • It isn't used to train AI models.
  • No one reads it unless you ask them to, to keep the network secure, or where the law requires it.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • To stop the network using a Google account, remove the account under Accounts in your Vault, or remove the network's access in your Google Account.
  • A removed account stays in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then it's deleted for good.
  • To have anything else deleted, ask the network's owner.
