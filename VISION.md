# Vision

I want:

1. Any AI I use, from anywhere, to do what I can do across all my devices and accounts.
2. Nothing to belong to one AI. If I switch, or use several, nothing is lost.
3. What it makes for me to be ready on any of my devices, with nothing to install or set up, for me and for the people I work with.

So:

- One place reaches everything I have, as me. My own devices are part of it, since some things only exist there.
- That place is worth stealing, so a break-in should get as little as possible: whoever stores or runs it can't read it, and whatever helps sees only what it's asked to do.
