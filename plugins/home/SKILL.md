---
name: home
description: Read before putting an app or a widget on a Home Screen page, arranging one, or answering what's on someone's.
---

Home keeps how each Home Screen page is arranged: `page` gives it, `arrange` changes it, `store` lists the apps that
could be added and `settings` the parts of Settings.

Its words:

- **Home Screen**: where apps and widgets sit, on its pages. A user has their own, and so does each group and the
  network. A user sees theirs, their groups' and the network's.
